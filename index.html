About

I'm a PhD student in Learning Sciences and Technologies at the University of Pennsylvania's Graduate School of Education. My research sits at the intersection of learning sciences and educational data mining — I design, develop, and evaluate AI-based instructional systems, and apply statistical and machine learning methods to mathematics and STEM education. I'm particularly interested in AI's role in K-12 and higher education in under-studied, non-WEIRD contexts.

Based in Philadelphia, PA.
