About

I'm a PhD Candidate in Learning Sciences and Technologies at the AIEd Lab, Penn GSE and a PennAI Fellow at University of Pennsylvania. My research sits at the intersection of learning analytics and AI in Education . I study Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) and AI-based instructional systems, and apply statistical and machine learning methods to understand K-12, and workforce learning.
